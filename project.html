Artificial Gravity & Planetary Engineering

A long-horizon research roadmap

This project started as a simple engineering question — can a compact device generate a localized gravitational effect — and grew into a multi-decade roadmap for what large-scale gravitational engineering could eventually make possible, up to and including reshaping a planet's orbit. Last year, I built and ran the first physical test of a small-scale prototype. That test is the reason the rest of this roadmap exists: it's the first data point in a much longer chain.

Core engineering directives

  • Field modulation scaling: moving from localized, small-scale gravitational effects to much larger drive arrays.
  • Resource-loop scaling: using off-world materials (asteroid and Martian resources) so infrastructure costs shift from linear spending to a self-sustaining, self-replicating cycle over time.
  • Planetary climate regulation: synchronized solar shade arrays to manage temperature and irradiance once orbital position changes.
